Frequently asked questions

Does this app show live bus locations?

No. It displays sample routes and static timetable details only.

Is the ticket valid for real travel?

No. This is a college demonstration system with fictional sample bus data.

How is the fare calculated?

Fare is based on the number of route segments, the bus's configured base fare, passenger category, and passenger count.
